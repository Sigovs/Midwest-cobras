About

Welcome to Midwest Cobras.

Everyone knows the iconic look of a Cobra. They turn heads everywhere they go.

Lifelong car enthusiast, Evan Kunard, has a deep passion for the classics. From frame-off restorations to owning his dream car, he now strives to help others make their dream car a reality!

At Midwest Cobras, we genuinely cherish your choice to invest in the car of your dreams. We dedicate ourselves to meticulous attention to every detail, employing a focused and passionate approach to help fulfill your aspirations.

We look forward to building long-lasting relationships with our customers, who become part of our Cobra family.
