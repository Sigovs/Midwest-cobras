    03 / Build

    Yours is not built yet.

    Every car leaves this shop specified by the person who is going to drive it. Color, stance, pipes, the stitching on the seat you will actually sit in — the list below is the conversation, in the order it happens.

    Build yours

    1. 01

      Model

      RT4 Classic or RT4B Black. Everything below follows from this one.

    2. 02

      Body and color

      Paint, stripes, and whether the car wears them at all.

    3. 03

      Wheels and stance

      Eighteen-inch rims, matte black among them, and what sits under the arches.

    4. 04

      Pipes and roll bars

      Ceramic-coated, side-exit. The two parts you hear and the two you grab.

    5. 05

      Interior

      Upholstery, stitching, gauges, wheel. The part you are actually sitting in.

    04 / Who we are

    Welcome to Midwest Cobras.

    Everyone knows the iconic look of a Cobra. They turn heads everywhere they go.

    Lifelong car enthusiast, Evan Kunard, has a deep passion for the classics. From frame-off restorations to owning his dream car, he now strives to help others make their dream car a reality!

    At Midwest Cobras, we genuinely cherish your choice to invest in the car of your dreams. We dedicate ourselves to meticulous attention to every detail, employing a focused and passionate approach to help fulfill your aspirations.

    We look forward to building long-lasting relationships with our customers, who become part of our Cobra family.
