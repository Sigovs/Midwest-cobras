04 Archive dossier

Forty

We focus entirely on putting people in cars that should not exist.

Midwest Cobras was born from a four-decade obsession with classic power-to-weight ratios. The original 1960s recipe—American V8 wedged into a lightweight British roadster chassis—was never meant for mass consumption. It was a beautiful anomaly.

Today, we preserve that ethos in partnership with Backdraft Racing. These are not retro replicas; they are modern, brutally fast performance roadsters engineered to be experienced with absolute sensory focus.
